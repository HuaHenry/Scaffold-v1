How the world works

Stone, timber, brick and roofing carry load. Unsupported blocks fall. Leaves, water and sand cannot support a building.

Doors occupy two cells. Place their bottom on a solid floor. Walls, windows, ladders and torches live on the background layer and must touch a solid block (or a connected wall); they cannot float, and vanish if their support is removed. Flowers need grass or dirt below.

Gravity OFF lets you design freely. Turn it back on to test your structure.

Build anywhere you can see. Walk through your house and make it feel like home.

Build / remove
Hold left / right mouse button and drag. Right-click erases solid blocks first. Hold Shift to erase the background wall.
Move / climb
A / D to walk, Space to jump, W / S on ladders. F toggles creative flight; use WASD to fly.
Doors
Select Oak door and click its bottom cell above a solid floor. Leave two cells free. E opens / closes a door near you.
Tools
B build, X erase, Q eyedropper, 1–9 quick select, G grid, Z undo, Y redo, P animated build.
Camera
Scroll to zoom. Middle-drag or arrow keys to pan. Home centers on you. The minimap jumps to another part of the meadow.
Recording
Record captures before/after states, background layers and actual actions / physics ticks. Export JSON downloads the session. Saved worlds stay in this browser.
